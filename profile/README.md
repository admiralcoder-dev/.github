## My Engineering Approach

I believe effective automation is more than a collection of test scripts. My approach emphasizes:

- **Risk-based coverage:** Prioritize critical user journeys, edge cases, and failures with real user impact.
- **Maintainable architecture:** Use reusable fixtures, Page Objects or appropriate alternatives, clear test data, and separation of concerns.
- **Reliable execution:** Design isolated, deterministic tests; avoid unnecessary waits and brittle selectors.
- **API-first validation:** Test service behavior, contracts, authentication, and negative scenarios at the right layer.
- **Continuous feedback:** Integrate automated checks, actionable reports, and diagnostics into CI/CD.
- **Responsible AI assistance:** Explore AI-supported test design and failure triage while keeping pass/fail assertions verifiable.
- **Accessibility and mobile quality:** Consider inclusive interfaces, responsive behavior, and device-specific risks.
